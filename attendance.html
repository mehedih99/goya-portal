Attendance Settings

These rules affect attendance calculations only; the main Admin/Staff Portal is untouched.

Automatic hourly shifts
Every 1 hour from the first shift through the last shift. No new shift is created after the last shift. Late = matched shift + grace period.

Live Attendance Workplace Verification

Web browsers cannot read a Wi-Fi SSID. Network verification uses the office public IP plus GPS radius.
STAFF SHIFT RULES

Live Shift & Break Controls

These switches directly control the Staff My Attendance page.

Allow Staff Split Shift ToggleWhen ON, staff can see the Split Shift ON/OFF control. When OFF, the control disappears and Rejoin is blocked.
Break Yes / No SelectionAllow staff to choose whether a break was taken.
Split Shift: EnabledBreak Selection: Enabled
Split Shift ON = staff can Rejoin after Check Out on the same operational day. Break Selection OFF = staff follows the Admin default and cannot change it.

Approved Workplace Locations

Approved Office Public IP / Network

Live shift starts are fixed every 1 hour from 8:00 AM through 5:00 PM. Late grace is 15 minutes. No new shift start is created after 5:00 PM.
